# POLY LOOT | DUNGEON SPEEDRUN V4

**Automated Halloween Dungeon Farming with 24-Hour Key Access**

Poly Loot Dungeon Speedrun V4 is designed to make farming the Halloween Dungeon 2026 faster and easier by automating combat, enemy targeting, room navigation, and loot collection.

## Features

- **Automatic Dungeon Entry** – Automatically enters the first room once the dungeon is ready.
- **Automatic Combat** – Detects enemies, teleports into attack range, and attacks using the Halloween Greatsword.
- **18-Room Dungeon Farming** – Progresses through all 18 rooms, including boss encounters.
- **Fast Room Transitions** – Teleports through unlocked doors to reach the next room.
- **Automatic Loot Collection** – Searches for nearby dropped items and attempts to pick them up before moving to the next room.
- **Automatic Reward Popups** – Closes the "NEW LOOT!" screens after boss encounters.
- **Manual Restart Support** – Automatically resumes farming after you manually restart the dungeon.
- **24-Hour Key System** – Access is unlocked for 24 hours after your key's first successful activation.
- **Simple Interface** – Includes ON/OFF controls, current room, enemy information, attack statistics, and remaining license time.

## Important: Dungeon Restart

**The script does NOT automatically press the "Run again" button.**

After completing all 18 rooms:

1. Wait for the **"Dungeon Cleared!"** screen.
2. Manually click **"Run again"**.
3. Wait for the new dungeon run to begin.
4. The script will reset its progress and automatically continue farming from Room 1.

You do **not** need to restart or re-execute the script between dungeon runs, provided it remains enabled and your access key is valid.

Each new run requires another Halloween Dungeon 2026 item.

## Requirements

- A valid 24-hour access key
- Halloween Dungeon 2026 access
- Halloween Greatsword equipped
- A compatible Roblox script executor

## How to Use

1. Execute the script.
2. Click **GET KEY** and complete the Linkvertise access process.
3. Copy your access key and paste it into the Key GUI.
4. Click **VERIFY KEY**.
5. Enter or prepare your Halloween Dungeon.
6. Enable **SPEEDRUN: ON**.
7. Let the script handle combat, room progression, and available loot.
8. After completing the dungeon, manually select **Run again** to start another run.

**Note:** The 24-hour timer begins when your key is first successfully activated. Performance and item collection may vary depending on game conditions, and uninterrupted farming is not guaranteed.
