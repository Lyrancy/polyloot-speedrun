# POLY LOOT | DUNGEON SPEEDRUN V5.5

**Automated Halloween Dungeon Farming with Auto Replay, Anti-AFK & 24-Hour Key Access**

Poly Loot Dungeon Speedrun V5.5 is designed to make farming the Halloween Dungeon 2026 faster, easier, and more efficient by automating combat, enemy targeting, room navigation, and loot collection.

Featuring a modern **OasisUI interface**, automatic weapon detection, optimized dungeon transitions, and an advanced Auto Replay system, the script aims to simplify repetitive dungeon farming.

## Features

- **Automatic Dungeon Entry** – Automatically enters the first room once the dungeon is ready.
- **Automatic Combat** – Detects enemies, teleports into attack range, and attacks using your currently equipped weapon.
- **Universal Weapon Detection** – Automatically recognizes equipped combat weapons without requiring a specific Greatsword.
- **18-Room Dungeon Farming** – Automatically progresses through all 18 rooms, including boss encounters.
- **Optimized Room Transitions** – Uses direct teleportation, position monitoring, and controlled retry attempts to improve room navigation.
- **Automatic Loot Collection** – Searches for nearby dropped items and attempts to collect them before proceeding to the next room.
- **Automatic Reward Popups** – Closes the "NEW LOOT!" screens after boss encounters.
- **Auto Replay (Beta)** – Attempts to restart the dungeon automatically after completing all 18 rooms.
- **Server-Confirmed Replay** – Monitors dungeon Scene events to verify successful restarts before continuing.
- **Anti-AFK** – Attempts to prevent inactivity disconnections during farming.
- **OasisUI Interface** – Modern interface with dungeon selection, feature toggles, and live statistics.
- **Executor Compatibility Detection** – Automatically selects available functions based on the user's executor.
- **24-Hour Key System** – Unlocks access for 24 hours starting from the key's first successful activation.

## Important: Auto Replay

**Auto Replay is currently in beta and may not work with every executor.**

After completing all 18 rooms:

1. Wait for the "Dungeon Cleared!" screen.
2. If Auto Replay is enabled, the script attempts to activate the **"Run again"** button automatically.
3. The script monitors server Scene events to confirm that the restart was accepted.
4. Once the new dungeon is ready, the script resets its progress and automatically continues farming from Room 1.

**If Auto Replay fails, manually click "Run again" to continue.**

You do not need to restart or re-execute the script between dungeon runs, provided the script remains enabled and your access key is valid.

**Note:** Every successful dungeon restart requires another Halloween Dungeon 2026 item.

## How to Use

1. Execute the script using a compatible executor.
2. Click **GET KEY** and complete the Linkvertise access process.
3. Copy your access key and paste it into the Key GUI.
4. Click **VERIFY KEY** to unlock the script.
5. Open the **Dungeon** tab in OasisUI.
6. Select **Halloween Dungeon**.
7. Equip your preferred compatible weapon.
8. Enable **Dungeon Speedrun**.
9. Optionally enable **Auto Replay** and **Anti-AFK**.
10. Let the script handle combat, room progression, and available loot collection.
